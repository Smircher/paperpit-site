Privacy policy

Paper Pit: Day Trading Academy · SayCO LLC · Effective November 14, 2026 (updated for ads on Android)

Paper Pit is a game made by SayCO LLC, a Kentucky limited liability company ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Paper Pit on Android or Windows, and when you visit this website.

The short version

  • We (SayCO LLC) don't collect or receive personal information from the game, and there are no accounts, analytics or crash reporting.
  • Everything the game saves stays on your device.
  • Android only: the free Android version shows a small banner ad on menu screens. The ads come from Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks, which can collect information from your device to show and measure ads. You choose personalized or non-personalized ads when you first start the game, and can change it at any time. The Windows version has no ads.

What the game stores on your device

  • Profiles: the profile names you type, lesson progress, your paper-money account, your trades and any journal notes you write.
  • Settings: choices such as which chart tools are shown, your ad privacy choice (Android), and that you have read the "it's a game" notice.
  • Live price recordings: if you play with live prices, the game saves one-minute price candles so the day can be replayed later.
  • An API key, if you enter one: kept in the game's local settings on your device.

None of this is sent to us. On Android it is removed when you uninstall the game. On Windows it is in your user profile's AppData\LocalLow\SayCO LLC\Paper Pit folder and in the game's local settings, and you can delete it at any time.

Live prices from Finnhub (optional)

Paper Pit works without an internet connection, apart from its optional live-price features. If you choose to enter a free API key from Finnhub.io to see live prices, the game asks Finnhub for prices directly from your device. Those requests include your API key and the ticker symbols you choose, and Finnhub can see your device's IP address, as with any internet request. Your Finnhub account and those requests are covered by Finnhub's privacy policy and terms of service. The key only reads prices; it does not connect to a broker or enable real trading.

Ads in the Android version

Paper Pit for Android is free and shows a small banner ad on menu screens only, never while you trade or answer a drill. The Windows version has no ads. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, which chooses ads from Unity Ads and other ad networks it works with.

To show ads, these companies receive information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads (for example, views and taps). They use it to choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under their own privacy policies, including Unity's privacy policy. SayCO LLC does not receive this information about you; we only see summary reports such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Your choices:

  • The first time you start the Android version, the game asks whether you want personalized or non-personalized ads. With non-personalized ads, we tell the ad networks that you don't consent to personalized advertising (GDPR) and that you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (US state privacy laws such as California's), so the ads you see are not based on information about you.
  • You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy on the lessons menu. The new choice applies to the next ads requested.
  • You can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Paper Pit is not directed at children, and its ads are not targeted at children.

App stores

If you get Paper Pit from Google Play or Steam, Google or Valve handle your purchase, download and store account under their own privacy policies. We may see the usual summary reports these stores give developers (for example, numbers of installs), which do not identify you to us.

This website

paperpit.app and saycogames.com are hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking on them. GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

Paper Pit is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date, before the version of the game that the change applies to is released.
